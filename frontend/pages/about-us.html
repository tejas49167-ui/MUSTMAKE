It all started with one thought.

My friend and I were always working out together and it was also part of our motivation or something we did, because after our PUC life ended we both joined different colleges; but the habit, it had not changed, we were texting on WhatsApp -> "Can we do workout now? Are you free?", and this pattern repeated for a year and more, one day I thought why can't I make an application that helps only us two to track our workouts initially only we both were using that app, but his exams came up, we decided not to use that app right at that time.

And at this time, I thought what if there were people like us, still doing exercises and maintaining discipline even when remote.

So I made a full-stack app that has individual accounts and connections, I spent more than a month to implement, improve, fix, make it more optimized, efficient and here I am.

If you truly love using this app, it's a good thing that this app is not only supporting us but also disciplined people and people like you.
