MUSTMAKE is an independent fitness tracking application designed to help users:

  • Record and manage workouts, track daily exercise activity and maintain workout streaks.
  • Monitor fitness progress and create and manage fitness profiles.
  • Connect with other members and compare workout activity through competitions.
  • Use optional AI-assisted exercise monitoring.

Features may change, improve or be discontinued over time.

It all started with on e thought.

My friend and I always worked out together. It was more than just exercise; it was part of our routine and a source of motivation for both of us. After our PUC days ended, we joined different colleges, but our habit of working out together never changed. We continued coordinating through WhatsApp, texting each other, "Can we work out now? Are you free?"

This continued for over a year. One day, I wondered why I couldn't build an application that would help just the two of us track our workouts and stay consistent, even when we were apart. Initially, the app was built exclusively for the two of us. However, when my friend's exams approached, we decided to take a break from using it.

That was when a bigger idea came to mind: What if there were thousands of people like us? People who wanted to stay disciplined, maintain their fitness routines, and motivate each other, even when distance kept them apart?

So I made a full-stack app that has individual accounts and connections, I spent more than a month to implement, improve, fix, make it more optimized, efficient and here I am.

If you truly love using this app, it's a good thing that this app is not only supporting us but also disciplined people and people like you.

Privacy policy

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use MUSTMAKE, we may collect the following information:

Account information

  • Full name, username and email address
  • >=6 ascii characters long password (stored as a secure password hash)
  • Google account identifier when using Google Sign-In

Profile information

  • Age, height, weight, profile picture and social media links,

except name other profile information is optional and can be updated through your account.

Workout information

When you record workouts, we collect information such as:

  • Exercise names, repetitions and sets completed
  • Weight used, exercise duration, distance or other exercise amounts, where applicable
  • Workout dates and timestamps

This information helps MUSTMAKE maintain your workout history, activity records, consistency streaks and progress.

Competition and security information

When you connect with other members through the competition feature, we store the connections between accounts and use relevant workout information to display daily activity and comparisons.

We may process information such as IP addresses, authentication attempts, verification records and session information to protect accounts, prevent misuse and maintain platform security. Verification codes are temporary and are stored using protected representations.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and maintain your account, and authenticate users through email verification and Google Sign-In.
  • Maintain workout records and exercise history; display user profiles and workout progress.
  • Provide competition and workout comparison features, and calculate workout streaks and activity statistics.
  • Support exercise monitoring features and improve application functionality and performance.
  • Detect suspicious activity, prevent unauthorized access and communicate essential account verification information.
  • Send daily workout reminder emails to account holders at the email address associated with their account when no workout has been recorded that day. Reminders are enabled by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings.

We do not use your fitness information to provide medical diagnoses.

Profile Visibility and Information Sharing Between Members

MUSTMAKE is designed as a community-based fitness tracking platform. An important part of MUSTMAKE is allowing logged-in members to view one another's fitness profiles and workout progress.

Other authenticated MUSTMAKE members may be able to view:

  • Your name and username, profile picture, age, height and weight
  • Your social media links and workout history

Members may also see relevant daily workout information through the competition feature.

This visibility is part of the platform's core functionality. Do not add information to your profile that you do not want other logged-in members to see.

MUSTMAKE profiles are intended for authenticated members, rather than being unrestricted public profiles accessible without an account.

Camera and AI Exercise Monitoring

MUSTMAKE provides optional AI-assisted exercise monitoring. When you choose to use this feature:

  • Your browser requests permission to access your camera.
  • Camera footage is processed locally in your browser for pose detection and exercise tracking.
  • The application does not request microphone access for this feature.
  • The reviewed application code does not upload or record the live camera feed.
  • When you switch away from the tab, MUSTMAKE stops the camera and pauses monitoring. When you return, it attempts to restart the camera and resume monitoring.
  • Workout results, such as repetitions and duration, may be saved to your account when you record a workout.

The application downloads MediaPipe libraries and pose-detection model resources from third-party services, including jsDelivr and Google Cloud Storage. Those providers may receive ordinary network request information, such as your IP address and browser information, when these resources are requested.

You can deny camera permission and continue using other available features.
