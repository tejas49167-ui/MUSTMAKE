How It Works

Get started with MUSTMAKE in just a few simple steps

1

Create Your Account

Sign up using your Google account or register with your email. Our secure authentication process ensures your data stays protected. Complete your profile setup to personalize your experience.

2

Log Your Workouts

Choose from our extensive exercise library or create custom workouts. Record your exercises, sets, reps, and weights. Our intuitive interface makes logging quick and easy.
